Dueloop privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Dueloop, the SLA timer add-on for Google Sheets ("Dueloop"), is published by Joao Duro De Matos, a sole proprietorship registered in Poland (NIP 9452193255), Ułanów 64D lok. 27, 31-460 Kraków, Poland ("we").

The short version

Dueloop runs inside Google's own Apps Script platform. We have no servers, and your spreadsheet data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Dueloop can only open the spreadsheet you use it in.

What Dueloop can access, and why

  • The current spreadsheet only (spreadsheets.currentonly): to read the columns you choose and write the SLA due, SLA left and SLA state columns and the row colours. It cannot open your other files.
  • Send email as you (script.send_mail): only the daily overdue email, only to the addresses you type in the settings (and, in Pro, the owner addresses in the column you pick). It cannot read your email.
  • Run while you are away (script.scriptapp): the hourly refresh and the refresh after an edit.
  • Show its sidebar (script.container.ui).
  • Connect to an external service (script.external_request): used only to check a Pro licence key with our payment provider, Polar (polar.sh). The call carries the key and nothing from your sheet.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings for each tracked tab (column names, goals, working hours, holidays, time zone, the email addresses you typed) are stored in the spreadsheet's own document properties, inside Google. Anyone who uses Dueloop in that spreadsheet can see them in the sidebar.
  • A Pro licence key and the result of its last check are stored in your own Apps Script user properties, inside Google.

We receive none of this. Stopping tracking or removing the add-on stops the processing; deleting the spreadsheet deletes the settings with it.

What we receive

Nothing from your spreadsheets. If you buy Pro, Polar is the seller and processes your payment under its own privacy policy; we receive from Polar your name, email address, country and order, to support your licence and to meet tax and accounting rules. Google may give us install counts and, if an error happens, technical logs from the Apps Script platform; an error message can mention a column name. We use these only to support and improve Dueloop.

Dueloop's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or let people read it.

Your rights

You have the rights the GDPR gives you: access, correction, deletion, objection, and a complaint to the Polish data protection authority (UODO). Write to us about anything we hold about you.
