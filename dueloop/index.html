Dueloop: SLA timers for Google Sheets

Dueloop turns any tracker sheet (support tickets, requests, tasks, invoices) into an SLA tracker. It counts working time only: your working days, your hours and your holidays.

Support: support@studiowebworks.com · Privacy policy · Terms of service

What it does

  • Adds three columns to your tab: SLA due, SLA left and SLA state.
  • States: On track, Due soon, Overdue, and for finished rows Met, Breached or Done.
  • Colours each row by its state (you can turn this off).
  • Recomputes every hour and whenever you edit the tab.
  • Sends a daily email with what is overdue or due soon, only when there is something to say.

Set it up

  1. Open your sheet, then Extensions › Dueloop › Set up SLA timers.
  2. Say how the due time is set:
    • Start date + one goal, for example 2d, 4h or 1d 4h;
    • Start date + goal by priority, with rules like High = 4h, one per line;
    • or an existing due date column. A due date without a time means the end of that day.
  3. Optional: the status column and the words that mean finished (Done, Closed), and a "finished on" date column to get Met or Breached.
  4. Set your working days, hours (or whole days only, or 24/7), holidays and time zone.
  5. Press Save and refresh.

Goals count working time: 2d is two working days, at the same time of day (opened Monday 15:00, due Wednesday 15:00). 4h is four working hours.

Columns are found by their header text, so you can move them. If you rename one, Dueloop tells you and waits until you pick it again.

Formulas

FormulaGives
=SLA_DUE(A2, "2d")The due date and time, two working days after A2 (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00).
=SLA_DUE(A2, "4h", "2026-12-25, 2026-12-26")The same, skipping those holidays.
=WORK_HOURS(A2, B2)Working hours between two dates, as a number.

Holidays can also be a range of dates, like =SLA_DUE(A2, "2d", Holidays!A2:A30). Google Sheets shows a formula's date without the time at first. To see the time too, pick the column and choose Format › Number › Date time.

Stop using it

In the sidebar press Stop tracking: the row colours go, and when no tab is tracked any more the hourly refresh and emails stop. The three SLA columns stay; delete them like any column. To remove the add-on entirely: Extensions › Add-ons › Manage add-ons.
