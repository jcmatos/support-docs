The short version

Slaloop runs on Atlassian Forge and is built for the "Runs on Atlassian" programme. It has no servers of its own. Everything it stores stays inside Atlassian's platform, for your Jira site, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else. It stores no work item summaries, descriptions or comments.

What Slaloop stores, and where

Slaloop keeps its data in the Forge SQL database that Atlassian hosts for the app on your site. Only Slaloop can read it; it is not shared with other sites or other apps.

  • Policies: name, JQL scope, statuses, goals, working hours, holidays and rules, as your administrators enter them. A rule can name a person, kept as an account id.
  • Clocks: for each work item and policy, the work item id and key, project key, status and priority ids, and the times: started, due, breached, stopped, time used.
  • Field values: the value of the "SLA status" and "SLA due" fields for each work item.
  • Rule log: which rule ran on which work item, when, and if it failed, the reason. That reason can quote Jira's own error message.

Slaloop also writes to Jira itself, through Jira's own APIs: the "SLA status" and "SLA due" fields, and whatever your rules say: comments, mentions, priority, assignee and status.

Finished results are removed 400 days after the work item stopped. When a work item is deleted, its clocks are deleted. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian removes the app's stored data under its Forge data retention rules. Export the report to CSV first if you want to keep it. Comments and other changes made by rules stay in Jira like any other Jira data.

Personal data

The only personal data Slaloop stores is the Atlassian account id of a person an administrator names in a rule. It stores no names or email addresses. To mention an assignee or reporter, it reads them from the work item at that moment and keeps nothing.

Once a week Slaloop reports the account ids it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API, as Atlassian requires. When Atlassian answers that an account has been closed, Slaloop removes that account id from the rule, and the rule reports a failure until an administrator chooses someone else.

Only Jira administrators can see and change policies. The report, the gadget and the panel show each person only the projects and work items they can see in Jira.

Atlassian processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy. For the data Slaloop stores, your organisation remains the controller; we never receive it.

What we receive

Nothing from your Jira content. Slaloop makes no calls outside Atlassian. Like every Marketplace partner, we can see the licensing and install information Atlassian provides to partners (for example which sites have the app installed and the licence tier) and the logs Forge keeps for the app. Those logs record counts and errors, and an error may contain a work item key. We use this information only to support and improve Slaloop.
