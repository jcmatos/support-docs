Support service level agreement

Publisher: Joao Duro De Matos (sole proprietorship), Kraków, Poland. Applies to Riskloop, Assetloop, Askloop and Exportloop. Last reviewed: 2 November 2026.

How to reach us

  • Email support@studiowebworks.com. Include the app, your site URL and what you expected to happen.
  • Security issues: security@studiowebworks.com. See each app's security page.

Hours of operation

Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Central European Time (Europe/Warsaw), except Polish public holidays. Email sent outside these hours is answered on the next business day.

Response targets

SeverityExampleFirst response
CriticalThe app is unavailable for all users, or data is lost or exposed1 business day
HighA main feature does not work and there is no workaround2 business days
NormalA bug with a workaround, or a question about how to use the app2 business days
LowFeature requests and suggestions5 business days

After the first response we keep you updated until the issue is resolved. Fixes ship as new app versions after code review and testing; Atlassian and Zendesk apps update automatically.

Availability

Riskloop, Assetloop and Askloop run only on Atlassian Forge, and Exportloop runs inside Zendesk. Their availability follows the platform's own; we run no servers of our own.

Scope

Support covers the current versions of the apps for customers with an active subscription or trial. It does not cover the Atlassian or Zendesk products themselves.
