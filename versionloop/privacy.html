The short version

Versionloop runs on Atlassian Forge and is built for the "Runs on Atlassian" programme. It has no servers of its own. Everything it stores stays inside Atlassian's platform, for your Jira site, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else. It never reads work item summaries, descriptions or comments.

What Versionloop stores, and where

Versionloop keeps its data in the Forge key-value store that Atlassian hosts for the app on your site. Only Versionloop can read it; it is not shared with other sites or other apps.

  • Sync groups: the name, the lead and follower projects (id, key and name), what syncs, the name filter and the delete settings, as your administrators enter them.
  • Links: which version or component in a follower belongs to which one in the lead, as ids.
  • Run status: when each group was last checked, and any problems, with the project, the item name and Jira's reason.
  • Audit log: each change the app made: when, the group, the project, the item, the old and new values of the synced fields, how it happened, and the account id of the person who changed the lead. Rows are removed after 365 days.

Versionloop also writes to Jira itself, through Jira's own APIs: it creates, updates and, only if your administrators turn deletes on, deletes versions and components in the follower projects, and moves work items to a replacement when it deletes one.

When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian removes the app's stored data under its Forge data retention rules. The versions and components it made stay in Jira like any other Jira data.

Personal data

The only personal data Versionloop stores is Atlassian account ids: the person who changed the lead, and a component lead, as they appear in the audit log. It stores no names or email addresses; names are shown by Jira when the page is opened.

Once a week Versionloop reports the account ids it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API, as Atlassian requires. When Atlassian answers that an account has been closed, Versionloop removes that account id from the audit log.

Only Jira administrators can open Versionloop. Atlassian processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy. For the data Versionloop stores, your organisation remains the controller; we never receive it.

What we receive

Nothing from your Jira content. Versionloop makes no calls outside Atlassian. Like every Marketplace partner, we can see the licensing and install information Atlassian provides to partners (for example which sites have the app installed and the licence tier) and the logs Forge keeps for the app. Those logs record errors, and an error may contain a project key, a version or component name, or Jira's own error text. We use this information only to support and improve Versionloop.
